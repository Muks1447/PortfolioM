## Application rules
- Keep the portfolio on the index route with anchor navigation because the requested experience is one continuous page.
- Share portfolio animation primitives and static project data in dedicated modules to keep sections reusable.
- Contact submissions open an email draft until a delivery service is configured; never report an email as sent.
